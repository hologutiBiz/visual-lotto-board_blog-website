OnimoLotto: An AI-Powered Lotto Assistant, Now in Beta

📅 October 2026 ⏱️ 3 min read 👤 Visual Lotto Board Team

Visual Lotto Board is announcing OnimoLotto, an AI-powered lotto assistant for forecast, analysis and results. It is still in Beta. This update explains what OnimoLotto is, who can use it today, and how we are approaching what comes next.

What is OnimoLotto?

OnimoLotto is an assistant designed to help you explore lotto forecast, pattern and results data from Visual Lotto Board. During Beta, it is built into the Lotto Forecast App dashboard. Instead of moving between screens to find information, you can ask a question in plain language and get an answer drawn from the data already available on the platform.

The aim is to make that data easier to explore and understand, especially for people who are still learning how the different tools fit together.

The Lotto Forecast App dashboard with the AI Assistant (Beta), powered by OnimoLotto

What OnimoLotto is not

OnimoLotto does not predict winning numbers, guarantee outcomes, or tell anyone what to play. Lotto draws are random, and no tool can change that. OnimoLotto is an exploration and explanation tool for the data and patterns the Lotto Forecast App tracks.

Please note: Pattern and analysis data describes what has happened in past draws. It does not predict or guarantee what will happen in future draws. Play responsibly and only within your means.

Who has access right now?

During Beta, OnimoLotto is available only to existing Lotto Forecast App subscribers, and only as a feature inside the app. It is not yet available to the general public.

We are starting with a smaller group so we can see how the assistant is used and improve it before opening it up more widely.

What we are working on

OnimoLotto is still in development and is updated regularly. Our focus is on refining how it understands questions, improving the clarity of its answers, and expanding what it can help users explore.

We plan to make OnimoLotto available to the wider public. When it is ready, we will announce it here and on our official channels. Until then, this page is the best place to follow progress.
